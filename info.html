ARTIST STATEMENT

i am a 21-year-old filmmaker based in new york city, drawn to the beauty of expressing universal human thought and feeling through film. themes of self-discovery, interiority, melancholy, quietness, hesitation, and ambiguity often guide my work. i have a deep interest in the tension between the spoken and unspoken– how silence and restraint can communicate something both beautiful and unsettling.

as someone who struggles to fully articulate my inner world through words alone, i have found solace in filmmaking. each film becomes an act of self-exploration of emotions, questions, and hidden parts of myself that i’m often hesitant to confront directly.

i begin with what is mine in the hope that it may become something we share.

what i see, what i feel, what we feel.
